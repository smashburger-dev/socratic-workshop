Model notes

This is one exact, one-dimensional quadratic with derivative 2(x − 3), a constant positive learning rate, and no noise. For this function, 0 < η < 1 converges, η = 1 oscillates at a fixed distance, and η > 1 diverges. Starting exactly at x = 3 stays there for every rate.

The run advances discrete steps every 700 ms, stops after 30 steps or at the graph boundary, and pauses whenever you change a parameter. The view is framed from the starting x and stays fixed through playback and learning-rate changes. Updates are instant, including with reduced motion enabled. This graph does not model other loss functions or multiple dimensions.
