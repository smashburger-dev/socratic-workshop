Model notes

This is the maximum steady-state rate of a serial line. Supply, demand, and intermediate buffers are sufficient. Stations are reliable, with no scrap, setup time, or downtime. It does not model startup, queue length, or the time one product spends in the line.

The station boxes show processing stages, not capacity by volume. Capacity is measured by the bars below. Output tokens represent rate, not a snapshot of inventory.
